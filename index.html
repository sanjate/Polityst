From Signals to Strategy

Organisations are operating in an environment shaped by technological disruption, geopolitical shifts, regulatory change, demographic transitions, and evolving societal expectations.

The challenge is often not access to information, but understanding which developments matter, what they signal about the future, and how organisations should respond.

My work focuses on translating policy, governance, and societal change into strategic insight by asking four questions:

1

What is changing?

2

What larger trend does it reveal?

3

What future scenarios become more plausible?

4

What capabilities may be needed to thrive under those conditions?

This combines policy analysis, systems thinking, and foresight to help organisations navigate uncertainty and prepare for emerging challenges and opportunities.

About

Founded by Sanja Terlević, Polityst helps organisations understand the policies, institutions and wider developments shaping their work, make informed decisions and contribute to the conversations and choices that affect their future.

I'm an independent policy analyst, advisor and writer with a background in economics, international development and public policy.

My professional experience spans regional development in Croatia, work with European institutions in Brussels, and research and analytical writing on public policy, economics, governance and societal change.

I'm particularly interested in how institutions and incentives shape our choices, how organisations respond to changing circumstances, and how knowledge and experience from different parts of society can contribute to better public decisions.

Through Polityst, I bring these interests together in commissioned analysis, strategic advisory and educational work. I also contribute analytical articles to media and specialist publications, making complex policy and economic developments more accessible to broader audiences.

My workshops on social entrepreneurship, public policy and advocacy reflect another dimension of the same mission: helping people and organisations develop the knowledge, confidence and practical methods to address societal challenges.

Through Polity Futures, I explore longer-term questions about possible futures, political imagination and the role of policy, institutions and collective action in shaping what comes next.

Across my work, I aim to combine analytical rigour with curiosity, practical usefulness and a commitment to public purpose.
